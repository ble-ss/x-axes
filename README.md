# x-axes

a political values quiz in the line of 8values, 9axes and 12axes. 50 axes in 8 families, 1200 statements, 51 badges, and a catalogue of 280 ideologies and single-cause schools.

**take it:** https://ble-ss.github.io/x-axes

## privacy

everything runs in your browser. there are no trackers, no cookies and no requests to other sites. your answers stay on your device. a share link keeps your result after the `#`, and a browser never sends that part to a server.

## this repo

this is the built site, ready to serve as static files. it has no build step and no dependencies.
